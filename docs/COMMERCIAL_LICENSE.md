This page explains when the MAMUT Engine commercial license is relevant. It contains no contractual text and is **not legal advice**. The binding terms will be those of the commercial license once it is drafted and reviewed by a lawyer.

## When the AGPL-3.0 is enough

The AGPL-3.0 allows commercial use, subject to its obligations. If you can comply with the AGPL-3.0, you do not need the commercial license.

## When the commercial license is needed

The commercial license is intended for those unable or unwilling to comply with the AGPL-3.0. Generic examples:

- Embedding the Engine in closed-source software that is distributed without its source code.
- Offering a modified version of the Engine, or software built on it, to users over a network without making the corresponding source code available to them.
- Distributing a modified version of the Engine without publishing those modifications under the AGPL-3.0.
- Any other use whose obligations under the AGPL-3.0 you do not intend to meet.

When in doubt, consult your own lawyer.
